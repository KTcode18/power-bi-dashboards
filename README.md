## Insights
- Total Sales across the dataset is 9.76M (USD), as shown on the KPI card, from 2.747K orders
- Sales by Year shows a rise from about 3M in 2018 to a peak of about 4.5M in 2019, followed by a sharp drop to about 1.4M in 2020
- Classic Cars is the top-selling segment, accounting for 39.81% of total sales by product line, more than double the next closest segment
- The Top 5 Countries by Sales chart shows the USA leading with approximately 3M in sales, followed by Spain (~1M), France (~1M), Australia (~0.6M), and the UK (~0.5M)
- There are 7 product/vehicle segments offered, as shown on the KPI card and seen in the 7 slices in the Sales by Product Line pie chart
- Based on the Correlation b/w Deal size and Discount % scatter chart, there is no strong linear correlation
- The Key Influencers visual identifies COUNTRY = Sweden as the strongest driver, making STATUS = Cancelled 17.16x more likely than average
