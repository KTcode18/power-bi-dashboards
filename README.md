## Insights
- Coffee & tea best selling products
- Similar Sales by number of transactions across all store locations
- Most busy time of day hour 08:00 & 10:00
- Highest sales by transaction count during months May & June
- Coffee & Tea sales account for most of the volume of total sales by value
